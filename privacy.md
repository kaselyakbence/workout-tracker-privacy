# Privacy Policy: Workout Tracker

**Last updated:** 3 November 2026

## 1. Who I am

Workout Tracker ("the app") is developed by Bence Kaselyák ("I", "me"). You can reach me at kaselyakbence.dev@gmail.com.

## 2. The short version

- The app has no accounts and no cloud. Your workouts, exercises, routines, weights and settings stay on your phone.
- I don't collect, receive or sell your data. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting of its own, and no advertising software.
- The app does not use the internet.

## 3. Data on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only on your device. It is never sent to me or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app removes it, and you can also clear it in Android's app settings.

The Export function creates a backup file that you save wherever you choose. I never receive it. Anyone who has the file can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.

## 4. Permissions

The app asks only for permission to show notifications, so the rest timer can alert you when it ends. It doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or the internet.

## 5. Sample ads

This is a test version. The banners and the "Support the developer" video you may see are **sample ads** drawn by the app itself, to try out the layout. They are labelled "Sample ad". No advertising company is involved, nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is earned. You can switch them off in the menu with "Remove ads".

## 6. Google Play

If you installed the app from Google Play, Google may collect technical information about the installation and about crashes under its own privacy policy, and may give me aggregated reports. I receive no information that identifies you. See <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

## 7. Your rights

I hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for me to show, correct or delete. You have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## 8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Security

I don't transmit your data, so there is no server holding it. Your phone's own protections (screen lock and device encryption) protect it.

## 10. Changes

If I change this policy, for example when real ads are added, I will post the new version here with a new date. For significant changes I will also say so in the app.

## 11. Contact
